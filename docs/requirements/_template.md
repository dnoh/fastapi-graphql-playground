number into its Requirements table. Keep the prompt's own sub-headings as
bold labels if it has them; never restart the numbering. Anything the interviewer
says out loud goes on the end, tagged `(verbal)`.

your questions are resolved in `/plan`'s ambiguity step; a rule the prompt forgot
becomes an inferred `I` row under the plan's Non-functional requirements.
