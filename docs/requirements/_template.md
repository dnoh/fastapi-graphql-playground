## Context

The ask, **verbatim**, as one continuous numbered list — `/plan` traces every
number into the task file's Traceability table. Keep the prompt's own sub-headings
as bold labels if it has them; never restart the numbering. Anything agreed out
loud rather than in writing — answers to your clarifying questions — goes on the
end, tagged `(verbal)`.

Nothing else lives here. Your decisions become Alternatives rows in the design doc;
your questions are resolved in `/plan`'s ambiguity step; a rule the prompt forgot
becomes an inferred `I` row in the task file's Traceability table.
